APPLIED AI

From idea to impact: building AI ventures that last

← All insights
Applied AI5 min read2026

Any team can bolt AI onto a product. Far fewer build businesses where AI changes the economics — how value is created, priced and defended. That's the difference between a feature and a franchise.

The first wave of AI products improved existing workflows. The lasting wave will do something harder: rebuild the business model underneath. When AI is genuinely core, it doesn't just make a product a little better — it opens markets that were never economically addressable, and it compounds advantage over time. We build deliberately for that.

The models only AI makes possible

Outcome-based value. When AI reliably delivers a result — risk avoided, time saved, better care — you can price for the outcome rather than for seats or licences. The customer pays for impact, and value compounds as the model improves.

Services-as-software. AI agents can now do work that once required costly expert teams, at software scale and margins. Whole markets — mid-market compliance, in-home care — become addressable for the first time.

Compounding data moats. A venture designed so its proprietary data and feedback loops make the AI sharper with every use turns an early lead into a durable advantage that incumbents struggle to close.

Speed is cheap and copyable. A business model that only works because of AI — and gets stronger the more it runs — is neither.

Why human judgement is the moat, not the obstacle

In the sectors we back, trust is the scarcest asset. Keeping humans accountable at the decisions that matter isn't a drag on the AI — it's what makes the product adoptable in regulated, high-stakes markets at all. Pairing human judgement with agentic speed is both the ethical choice and the commercial one.

That is how CSG builds: validate a real problem, design the AI-native model, keep people in the loop, and bring the capital and commercial discipline to scale it. Idea to impact, built to last.
