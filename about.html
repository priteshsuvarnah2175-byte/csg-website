ABOUT CSG

A group built to build.

Cykus Systems Group is a venture studio and builder. We create and scale responsible businesses where human judgement and AI together change how an entire sector works.

We are not a fund that writes cheques and waits. CSG is an operating group — we conceive ventures, build them, back them with capital and expertise, and stay in the room as they scale.

Cykus Systems Group brings together operators, builders and technologists with deep experience across technology, sustainability, healthcare and enterprise transformation. We start companies from first principles, partner with exceptional founders, and give every venture the capital, engineering and commercial muscle it needs to reach real scale.

Our conviction is simple: the most durable businesses of the next decade will be built where human ingenuity and judgement meet the speed and intelligence of AI — and they will be built responsibly, in the sectors society most depends on. That is why we focus on Health and Business Resilience, and why every CSG venture is built to be responsible by foundation: accountable, transparent and trusted. Responsible businesses earn their place and go further — and we build them to last.

How we operate

We work hands-on, from zero to scale. We validate a real problem, build an AI-enabled product around it, keep humans accountable at every decision that matters, and design commercial models that only AI makes possible. Then we bring the capital, partnerships and go-to-market discipline to turn a product into a company.

We also put that capability to work for others. Alongside building our own ventures, CSG offers venture building, consulting and digital and AI-platform services to organisations creating ventures of their own — which is how we fund the group and sharpen what we build.

Our work has been recognised by Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub — a signal that the problems we choose, and the responsible way we build, stand up to serious scrutiny.
