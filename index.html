OUR VENTURES
Building solutions for real-world challenges.

We build and back ventures that pair human judgement with the intelligence of AI — in the places where that combination is set to reshape how a whole sector works. In Health, AI is shifting care from reactive to preventive — spotting problems earlier, easing pressure on carers, and helping people live independently and with dignity for longer. These are shifts that change real outcomes — for patients and families, and for the businesses communities depend on. It's a portfolio built to grow as AI opens new frontiers across both.

AgeTech Health
Cegga
Ageing at home, with independence.

Cegga is an AI-powered Support-at-Home platform that helps senior Australians live independently and safely at home. Cutting-edge, non-intrusive sensors — no cameras — read everyday well-being patterns, so subtle changes that may signal risk or cognitive decline are seen before they become problems, turning reactive care into proactive care. The result: reassurance and visibility for families, earlier action for carers, and real efficiencies for Australian home-care providers.

Climate Tech Business Resilience
Clarius
Prepared by agents. Signed off by humans.

Clarius is an agentic AI platform for mandatory climate reporting, built for Australian mid-market companies. It brings together human judgement and the speed and intelligence of AI agents: the agents run the full ASRS obligation — gap analysis, Scope 1 & 2 emissions, physical and transition risk and scenario analysis — while your team signs off at every gate that matters, keeping every figure assurance-ready and ASIC-lodgement ready. From that trusted climate data, Clarius is building the bridge to sustainable finance — turning compliance into access to green and sustainability-linked capital.

OUR EDGE · AI BUSINESS MODELS
Built for value and stability — not the hype cycle.

AI is moving faster than any technology before it, and much of what is being built today will prove to be froth. We take the opposite stance — building ventures on durable foundations: real economics, defensible advantages, and models designed to keep creating value long after the hype settles.

Value you can measure

The strongest businesses are paid for outcomes, not promises. We build ventures whose value is concrete and provable — so customers keep paying because the result is real, and that value compounds as the system improves.

Services as software

Across every industry, expert work that was once scarce, slow and costly is becoming software. We build the platforms that deliver that expertise reliably and at scale — meeting demand that organisations cannot simply hire their way to, with a consistency human teams alone can't sustain.

Moats that compound

In a market where features are copied in weeks, durability is the only real edge. We design for advantages that compound — proprietary data, earned trust, and systems that get smarter with every use — so our ventures strengthen over time instead of fading with the cycle.

Responsible AI Foundation

Responsible isn't a constraint on what we build — it's why it lasts. Every CSG venture keeps people accountable for the decisions that matter, protects those it serves and the data it touches, and earns trust by being transparent and defensible. In the regulated, high-stakes markets we work in, that isn't a nice-to-have — it's the ground durable value is built on. Responsible businesses go further, and we build them to go the distance.
